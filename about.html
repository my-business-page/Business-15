04 / THE APPROACH

Be present.
Stay curious.

Kiku of Dollz & Cupcakes describes the work as pure, simple and organic. The camera observes rather than constantly directing, leaving room for people to interact naturally.

That means watching the edges of an event too: a nervous hand, a friend laughing off-camera, a parent fixing a collar, a room changing as guests arrive.
